Power Electronics, Designed for Real-World Grids

VQNIX is a technology-driven company focused on designing power-electronic systems that work reliably under real grid conditions, dynamic loads, and long-term field operation.

We build inverters, solar hybrid PCUs, EV chargers, MPPT platforms, grid-tie inverters, and static voltage stabilizers — all engineered in-house from concept to field behavior.

Engineering at VQNIX

At VQNIX, we believe reliable power systems are designed — not assembled. In a market driven by reference designs and integration kits, we focus on owning the engineering behind every platform we build.

  • In-house design of power stages, control algorithms, and PCB layouts
  • Deep focus on grid interaction, EMI, thermal behavior, and protection
  • Platforms engineered for worst-case grids and dynamic loads
  • Ability to debug, adapt, and evolve designs over their lifecycle

Our Product Platforms

  • Low-voltage inverter platforms (12V / 24V / 48V)
  • Solar hybrid PCUs and hybrid inverter boards

Why VQNIX

Modern electrical systems operate in environments that are far from ideal — weak grids, voltage disturbances, thermal stress, and nonlinear loads are the norm, not the exception.

VQNIX designs power systems with these realities in mind. Our platforms prioritize stability, protection, and reliability over headline specifications and nominal ratings.
