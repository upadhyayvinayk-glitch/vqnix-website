About VQNIX

VQNIX is a technology-driven company focused on the design and development of power-electronic systems for real-world electrical environments.

We specialize in inverters, solar hybrid PCUs, MPPT platforms, EV chargers, grid-tie inverters, and static voltage stabilizers, with strong emphasis on design ownership and engineering depth.

We own the complete engineering lifecycle — from power-stage architecture and control algorithms to PCB layout, EMI, thermal behavior, and long-term field reliability.
