Product Overview

VQNIX IGBT Static Voltage Stabilizers provide instantaneous voltage correction using PWM-controlled IGBT power stages. Unlike servo stabilizers, these systems have no moving parts, ensuring high reliability and fast response for critical industrial loads.

Key Features

  • Correction time < 10 ms
  • Wide input voltage range
  • High efficiency (> 97%)
  • No mechanical components
  • Microcontroller-based digital control
  • Over-voltage, under-voltage & short-circuit protection
