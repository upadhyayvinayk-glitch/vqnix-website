About VQNIX

VQNIX is a technology-driven company focused on the design and development of power-electronic systems for real-world electrical environments. We specialize in inverters, solar hybrid PCUs, MPPT platforms, EV chargers, grid-tie inverters, and static voltage stabilizers.

Unlike solutions built from reference designs or integration kits, VQNIX owns the complete engineering lifecycle — from power-stage architecture and control algorithms to PCB layout, EMI behavior, thermal performance, and long-term field reliability.
