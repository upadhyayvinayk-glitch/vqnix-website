About VQNIX

VQNIX is a technology-driven company focused on the design and development of power-electronic systems for real-world electrical environments. We specialize in inverters, solar hybrid PCUs, MPPT platforms, EV chargers, grid-tie inverters, and static voltage stabilizers.

Unlike products built around reference designs or integration kits, VQNIX owns the complete engineering lifecycle — from power-stage architecture and control algorithms to PCB layout, thermal behavior, EMI performance, and long-term field reliability.

Engineering at VQNIX

  • In-house design of power stages, control algorithms, and PCB layouts
  • Deep focus on grid interaction, EMI, thermal behavior, and protection
  • Platforms engineered for worst-case grids and dynamic loads
  • Ability to debug, adapt, and evolve designs over their lifecycle

Our Product Platforms

Low-Voltage Inverter Platforms

12V, 24V, and 48V inverter architectures designed for high current stress, surge handling, and continuous operation.

Solar Hybrid PCU Platforms

Intelligent power-management systems coordinating solar, battery, grid, and load under continuously changing conditions.

MPPT Control Platforms

Low-voltage and high-voltage MPPT platforms engineered for stable operation across wide solar input ranges.

Static Voltage Stabilizers
