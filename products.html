Our Products & Engineering Platforms

VQNIX develops power-electronic platforms designed for real-world electrical conditions — fluctuating grids, dynamic loads, thermal stress, and long-term field operation. Below is an overview of our key product categories and the engineering principles behind them.

Low-Voltage Inverter Platforms (12V / 24V / 48V)

Low-voltage inverters operate under extreme current stress. Designing these systems requires careful attention to conductor sizing, thermal margins, device derating, and control stability.

VQNIX inverter platforms scale intelligently across voltage levels, maintaining a consistent design philosophy while adapting current paths, switching losses, and thermal behavior.

  • 12V inverters optimized for very high current and surge conditions
  • 24V platforms balancing current reduction and efficiency
  • 48V platforms enabling higher power density and improved thermal margins
  • Stable PWM control under nonlinear and transient loads
  • Protection against overload, short-circuit, and abnormal battery conditions

Solar Hybrid PCU Platforms

A solar hybrid PCU is a real-time power management system. It must continuously coordinate solar generation, battery charging, grid availability, and load demand without interruption.

VQNIX solar hybrid PCUs are designed with a battery-centric architecture, robust inverter stages, and stable digital control loops to handle unpredictable operating conditions.

  • Seamless coordination between solar, battery, grid, and load
  • Support for 12V, 24V, and 48V battery systems
  • High-surge inverter stages with conservative device derating
  • Digital control with comprehensive protection logic
  • Thermal design suitable for continuous-duty operation

MPPT Charge Controller Platforms

Maximum Power Point Tracking is not just about efficiency at nominal conditions. Real MPPT systems must remain stable across wide solar voltage ranges, fast irradiance changes, and varying battery states.

VQNIX MPPT platforms are engineered for both low-voltage and high-voltage solar architectures, supporting flexible system design without compromise on protection or reliability.

  • True MPPT algorithms with continuous tracking
  • Wide solar input voltage compatibility
  • Support for high charge currents and battery voltages
  • Selectable switching frequencies for efficiency optimization
  • Protection against short-circuit, over-temperature, and abnormal conditions

EV Charger Power Platforms (LLC & PFC+LLC)

EV chargers are high-stress power systems operating at the intersection of grid compliance, efficiency, thermal performance, and safety. Poorly designed chargers fail under real grid conditions and repeated cycling.

VQNIX EV charger platforms use high-frequency resonant topologies with robust front-end power factor correction where required, ensuring efficiency, stability, and long-term reliability.

  • LLC and PFC+LLC architectures for wide input operation
  • Designed for 48V, 60V, and 72V battery systems
  • Stable operation under weak or distorted grids
  • Comprehensive protection and fault handling
  • Thermal design optimized for repeated charge cycles

Grid-Tie Inverters (GTI)

Grid-tie inverters must synchronize precisely with grid voltage, frequency, and phase while maintaining stability under disturbances. Poor grid quality exposes weaknesses in control-loop and sensing design.

VQNIX GTI platforms focus on robust grid interaction, accurate sensing, and stable control behavior under real distribution networks.

  • Accurate grid synchronization and sensing
  • Stable current injection under grid disturbances
  • Protection against abnormal grid conditions
  • Designed for long-term operation in real installations

Static Voltage Stabilizers

Mechanical servo stabilizers were designed for slow voltage drift. Modern industrial and electronic loads generate voltage disturbances that occur within milliseconds.

VQNIX static voltage stabilizers use fully electronic, IGBT-based AC–AC PWM architectures to deliver deterministic, high-speed voltage correction without mechanical delay.

  • Response time within one AC cycle
  • No servo motor or moving parts
  • Continuous voltage correction
  • Designed for nonlinear and transient loads
  • Industrial-grade thermal and protection design

Control Cards & Power Management Electronics

In complex power systems, control electronics define stability, protection behavior, and adaptability. VQNIX develops dedicated control cards for inverters, MPPT systems, UPS platforms, and battery management applications.

  • Inverter and UPS control card platforms
  • High-voltage MPPT control cards
  • Battery management systems (BMS)
  • Designed for integration into custom power architectures
