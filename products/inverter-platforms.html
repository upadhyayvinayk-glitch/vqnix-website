Low-Voltage Inverter Platforms

12V / 24V / 48V inverter architectures designed for high-power reality.

Product Overview

Low-voltage inverters operate under extreme electrical stress. At these voltage levels, current magnitude, thermal limits, and transient behavior define long-term reliability.

VQNIX inverter platforms are engineered with a consistent high-power design philosophy, scaled intelligently across 12V, 24V, and 48V battery systems.

Engineering Focus

  • High-current power stage design with conservative device derating
  • Optimized copper paths and current sharing
  • Low stray inductance PCB layouts
  • Thermal-first design for continuous duty operation
  • Stable PWM control under surge and nonlinear loads

Platform Variants

  • 1 KVA – 12V inverter for heavy current and startup surge
  • 2.5 KVA – 24V inverter balancing efficiency and current stress
  • 4 KVA – 48V inverter with improved power density and thermal margin

Typical Applications

  • Home and commercial backup systems
  • Battery-based power systems
  • Hybrid inverter platforms
  • High-surge and dynamic load applications
