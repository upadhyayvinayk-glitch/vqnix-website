Solar Hybrid PCU

Power management engineered for unpredictable real-world conditions.

A solar hybrid PCU is a dynamic power-management system. It must coordinate solar generation, battery storage, grid interaction, and load demand without interruption.

  • Seamless source transition without load drop
  • Battery-centric design (12V / 24V / 48V)
  • Robust inverter stage with conservative derating
  • Digital control with comprehensive protection
  • Thermal design for continuous-duty operation

Applications

  • Residential solar + battery backup
  • Small commercial hybrid systems
  • Unreliable grid environments
