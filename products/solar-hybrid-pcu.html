Solar Hybrid PCU Platforms

Power management engineered for unpredictable real-world conditions.

What Is a Solar Hybrid PCU?

A solar hybrid PCU is a real-time power management system. It must continuously coordinate solar input, battery charging, grid availability, and load demand without interruption.

Engineering Architecture

  • Battery-centric system design
  • Seamless transition between solar, battery, and grid
  • Robust inverter stage rated for surge and overload
  • Digital control loops for voltage and current regulation
  • Thermal design optimized for continuous operation

Typical Applications

  • Residential solar + battery backup
  • Small commercial power systems
  • Areas with weak or unreliable grids
  • Solar-priority uninterrupted power applications
